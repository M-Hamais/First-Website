Web Development as a career

A career as a web developer can be rewarding. Web developers build and maintain websites and web applications. It involves skills like coding in languages such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and knowledge of web frameworks, databases, and server-side technologies. To start, you can learn these skills through online courses, bootcamps, or formal education. Building a portfolio of projects is crucial to showcase your skills to potential employers or clients. Web development offers diverse opportunities in various industries and can lead to roles like front-end developer, back-end developer, full-stack developer, or specialized positions like UI/UX designer or DevOps engineer. Continuous learning is key in this field due to the ever-evolving technology landscape.
